TypeTune lexicon notice
=======================

lexicon.json (English and Russian word sets) is a derived, modified work:
words were selected by frequency, filtered by spelling validity and merged with
independently authored entries by scripts/build_lexicon.py. It is distributed under

(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The typos and ё tables are
independently authored.
